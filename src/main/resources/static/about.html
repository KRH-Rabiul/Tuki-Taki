🌉 My story

Tuki-Taki — your campus's own marketplace

Buy, sell, and connect with people on your campus. No shipping fees, no strangers off-campus — just a safer, simpler way to trade with people you already share a campus with.

THE PROBLEM I'M SOLVING

Campus buying and selling shouldn't be this hard

Every semester, students end up with textbooks, gadgets, and furniture they no longer need — while other students on the same campus are searching for exactly those things. Group chats and scattered posts make it slow and unsafe to connect.

Tuki-Taki brings everyone onto one platform built specifically for campus life — one place to list, chat, and meet up, instead of a dozen scattered group chats.

📚Textbooks & notes
💻Gadgets & electronics
🪑Furniture & dorm gear
👕Clothes & more
HOW IT WORKS

Three simple steps

1

Browse or list

Search for what you need, or post your own item in a couple of minutes with a photo and a price.

2

Chat and agree

Message the buyer or seller directly to confirm details, condition, and pickup time.

3

Meet and collect

Collect your order safely at our fixed campus pickup point — no need to share your home address.

WHY IT'S SAFE

Built with trust in mind

🎓
Campus-only accounts

Everyone here is a member of the same campus community, not an anonymous stranger from the internet.

📍
Fixed pickup point

Every order is collected at one verified, well-lit campus location — never a private address.

🚩
Reporting & moderation

Every listing can be reported, and new listings are reviewed before they go live.

✅
Verified sellers

Sellers with a blue checkmark have been manually reviewed and verified.
